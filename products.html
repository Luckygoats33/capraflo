CapraFlo · Locked

CapraFlo growth OS

The problem

DTC brands assemble Appstle or PayWhirl for subscriptions, Smile for points, Refersion for creators, then a separate bundle or discount app. Each one owns a slice of Admin, a slice of the theme, and a monthly line item. Checkout stays Shopify — the stack around it does not.

How it works

CapraFlo is one embedded Partner app. Site modes pick the money path. CapraFlo AI stages selling plans and automatic discounts. You agree. GraphQL syncs them. Theme blocks and the /apps/capraflow proxy read the active mode. Bundles, Subscribe & Save, Loyalty, and Affiliate stay in one hub.

CapraCred · Proposed

CapraCred

The problem

Review apps are a second design system on the PDP. They fight CapraFlo’s subscribe and bundle blocks, and they add another $30–40 to the monthly stack.

How it works

CapraCred is proposed as a focused reviews companion: moderation in a Capra-styled Admin, a PDP widget, and UGC capture that sits beside CapraFlo theme blocks instead of covering them. Not sold as a live App Store listing.

CapraBoost · Proposed

CapraBoost

The problem

Thank-you upsell apps interrupt a finished purchase or require a checkout extension the rest of the stack does not share. AOV tools become a fifth vendor.

How it works

CapraBoost is proposed as a thank-you / checkout UI extension: one warm offer after the order is paid. Aligned with the active CapraFlo Site mode. Shoppers can add or skip. The purchase itself already happened on native checkout.
