How Capra Suite works

One money path from install to thank-you. CapraFlo locks the growth OS. CapraCred and CapraBoost complete the journey as proposed companions. Checkout stays native Shopify.

1

Install CapraFlo

Install CapraFlo as an embedded Shopify Admin app. Dashboard, Bundles, Subscriptions, Loyalty, Affiliate, Site modes, and Settings appear in the left nav — ready for the money path.

CapraFlo · Locked
Shopify AdminApps → CapraFlo installed
Embedded navBundles · Subscribe · Loyalty · Affiliate
2

CapraFlo AI preview

CapraFlo AI scans catalog and storefront posture, then stages a concrete preview: which Site mode fits, which selling plans and discount tiers would apply, and what the merchant will see before anything writes to Shopify.

CapraFlo AI · Locked
Catalog scanProducts, variants, collections
Staged previewMode + plans + discounts · no writes yet
3

Site mode — Yes, I agree

Pick DTC Growth, Mobile Conversion, Subscribe-Led, Loyalty-Led, or Creator / Affiliate-Led. Confirm with Yes, I agree. CapraFlo creates local Bundle, Subscribe & Save, Loyalty, and Affiliate records from the template.

Site modes · Locked
Yes, I agreeDTC Growth applied
Local recordsBundles · S&S · Loyalty · Affiliate
4

Selling plans + automatic discounts

CapraFlo syncs selling plan groups and automatic basic discounts through Admin GraphQL. Theme embeds and product/cart blocks read the active mode. Checkout stays native Shopify — selling plans and discounts, not a parallel checkout.

Sync · Locked
Selling plan groupsAdmin GraphQL sync
Automatic discountsVolume & mix tiers live
5

CapraCred on the PDP

CapraCred puts trusted reviews and social proof on the product page — the moment shoppers decide. Proposed as a focused reviews app in the Capra Suite, designed to sit beside CapraFlo without fighting the theme.

CapraCred · Proposed
★★★★★ 4.9Widget + moderation on the PDP
Theme-friendlyDesigned beside CapraFlo blocks
6

CapraBoost on thank-you

After checkout, CapraBoost surfaces a thank-you upsell — one more offer while the buyer is still warm. Proposed checkout UI / thank-you extension that compounds average order value without interrupting the purchase itself.

CapraBoost · Proposed
+ Add-on offerSingle warm offer on thank-you
